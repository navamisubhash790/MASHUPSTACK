India Meteorological Department Alert to Kerala

Thiruvananthapuram: The India Meteorological Department (IMD) has issued an orange alert in ten districts of of Kerala on Friday, warning of the possibility of very heavy rainfall at isolated places. The alert covers Thiruvananthapuram, Kollam, Pathanamthitta, Alappuzha, Kottayam, Idukki, Kozhikode, Wayanad,Kannur and Kasaragod districts. A yellow alert has been issued in the remaining four districts: Ernakulam trissur,palakkad and malappuram.
