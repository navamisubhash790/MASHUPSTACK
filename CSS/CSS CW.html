The European Council is the EU institution that brings together national leaders to set the overall political direction and priorities of the European Union. Led by President António Costa, it is composed of the heads of state or government of the 27 EU member states, alongside the President of the European Commission. Rather than passing direct laws, the European Council provides strategic impetus, guides major foreign and security policy, and shapes the long-term agenda of the Union through consensus-based summit conclusions
