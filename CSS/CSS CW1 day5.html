Visiting Euorope for the first time

Favorite destination.

Do not try to see the entire continent in one week. Focus on 2 to 3 countries or a classic route like Paris, the Swiss Alps, and Rome. Moving around too often wastes precious vacation time.Check if you need a Schengen Visa ahead of time, especially if you are traveling from India. Apply several months early and keep digital and printed copies of your passport, hotel bookings, and travel insurance

European streets, train stations, and historic hotels feature plenty of stairs and old cobblestones. Pack a single carry-on backpack or a light suitcase that you can easily lift yourself.Trains connect major cities quickly and comfortably, while walking is the best way to explore neighborhoods. Pack a reliable pair of comfortable walking shoes and download navigation apps like Google Maps before you go
